Ball of Air

School conjuration (creation) [air]; Level cleric/oracle 0, druid 0

CASTING

Casting Time 1 standard action
Components V, S

EFFECT

Range close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Effect 5-ft. radius ball of air
Duration 1 round
Saving Throw Reflex negates; Spell Resistance no

DESCRIPTION

You send a small globe of air at the target. As the air ball hits him, the target must make a Reflex save or be knocked off balance for 1 round, losing his Dexterity bonus to AC. This spell has no effect on individuals with the uncanny dodge class feature*.
