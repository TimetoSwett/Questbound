Ring of Protection

Aura faint abjuration; CL 5th; Slot ring; Price 2,000 gp (+1), 8,000 gp (+2), 18,000 gp (+3), 32,000 gp (+4), 50,000 gp (+5); Weight —

DESCRIPTION

This ring offers continual magical protection in the form of a deflection bonus of +1 to +5 to AC.

CONSTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS

Feats Forge Ring; Spells shield of faith; Special creator must be of a level at least three times the bonus of the ring; Cost 1,000 gp (+1), 4,000 gp (+2), 9,000 gp (+3), 16,000 gp (+4), 25,000 gp (+5)
