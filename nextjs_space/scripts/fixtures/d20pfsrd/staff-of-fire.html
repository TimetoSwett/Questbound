Staff of Fire

Aura moderate evocation; CL 8th; Slot none; Price 18,950 gp; Weight 5 lbs.

DESCRIPTION

Crafted from bronzewood with brass bindings, this staff allows use of the following spells:

  • Burning hands (1 charge)
  • Fireball (2 charges)
  • Wall of fire (3 charges)

CONSTRUCTION REQUIREMENTS

Craft Staff; Spells burning hands, fireball, wall of fire; Cost 9,475 gp.
