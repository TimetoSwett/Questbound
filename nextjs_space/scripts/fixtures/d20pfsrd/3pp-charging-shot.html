Charging Shot (Combat)

Prerequisite(s): Fast movement class feature

Benefit(s): You can charge towards an opponent and make a ranged attack. The charge works exactly like charging with a melee weapon (including bonuses and penalties) except that you must end your movement at least 10 feet away from the nearest square threatened by the intended target.
